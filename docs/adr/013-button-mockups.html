Songlist Button Mockups

On the Karaoke Underground songlist, the column names Artist, Title and Album sort the list, but they don’t look like they can be tapped. The site also has no written rule for what a button looks like. These are the options John asked for on 2026-09-27: ten for the list buttons and ten for the column names, from minimal to maximal, after a first round of four.

Pick one from each set, like lists 3 and sort 5. The choice goes into ADR-013 as the site’s button rule.

What every option keeps

  • White on black, as the owner asked, with no white panels. A near-black fill is fine.
  • Touch targets at least 44px tall, and control borders at 3:1 or more on black.
  • The pick shows by a shape, like a bar, an X, a circle or a stamp, so it never relies on brightness alone and needs no hover.
  • The controls underneath stay the same: radio buttons for the lists and pressed buttons for the sort, so screen readers hear what they do now.
  • A new font costs 15 to 40 KB on phones, which load none today, plus a licence note. Lists 1, 3, 7 and 9, and sort 1, 4, 5, 6 and 8, need no new font.
  • Outlined type (lists 8, sort 10) and heavy tilts (lists 4 and 10) need a check at arm’s length on a phone in a dark bar.
