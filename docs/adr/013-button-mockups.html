Songlist Button Mockups

On the Karaoke Underground songlist, the column names Artist, Title and Album sort the list, but they don’t look like they can be tapped. The site also has no written rule for what a button looks like. These are the options John asked for on 2026-09-27 and 28, each set from minimal to maximal: a first round of four, ten for the list buttons, ten for the column names, ten more for the column names as pills, and four ways for pill 2 to show which part of a song is which.

Pick a list button and a column name, like lists 3 with sort 5 or pill 4. The choice goes into ADR-013 as the site’s button rule.

What every option keeps

  • White on black, as the owner asked, with no white panels. A near-black fill is fine.
  • Touch targets at least 44px tall, and control borders at 3:1 or more on black.
  • The pick shows by a shape, like a bar, an X, a circle or a stamp, so it never relies on brightness alone and needs no hover.
  • The controls underneath stay the same: radio buttons for the lists and pressed buttons for the sort, so screen readers hear what they do now.
  • A new font costs 15 to 40 KB on phones, which load none today, plus a licence note. Lists 1, 3, 7 and 9, sort 1, 4, 5, 6 and 8, and pills 1 to 9 need no new font.
  • Outlined type (lists 8, sort 10) and heavy tilts (lists 4 and 10) need a check at arm’s length on a phone in a dark bar.

Column names as pills

Artist, Title and Album as pills, the rounded shape phone apps use for things you tap, with the list sorted by Artist in each. If a pill wins, the list buttons and the site’s other buttons round off to match.

Pill 2 as a key to the songs

On a phone each song takes two lines: the first two columns on the top line, with a dash between them, and the third below in grey. Today’s column names pile up the same way, so they read as a key to every song. Pills in one row lose that. Here are four ways to keep it with pill 2, each sorted by artist and then after a tap on Title. On a wide screen, where each column has its own place, any of them simply heads its column.

Pairs that go together

  • Label-maker tape: lists 3 with sort 3.
  • Poster type: lists 8 with sort 10.
  • Cut and paste: the ransom note, lists 4, with the rubber stamp, sort 9.
  • Patches: lists 9 with pill 8.
  • Drawn by hand: the sprayed circle, lists 5, with the marker-drawn pills, pill 10.
